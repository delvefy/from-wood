From Wood — Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 3, 2026

You can delete your From Wood account and all associated data yourself, either in the app or from any web browser.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the game — in the app, or on the web at delvefy.github.io/from-wood — and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. In the Danger zone, choose Delete account and confirm with your password.

What gets deleted

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your email address and login credentials, your game progress and saves, and your tournament scores and history. Purchase transaction records are retained by Google Play or the Apple App Store according to their own policies; we keep no other data after deletion.

Can't sign in?

If you can no longer access your account, email us at CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER@example.com from the address you registered with and we will delete the account for you.

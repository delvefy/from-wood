From Wood — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

From Wood is an idle crafting game. This policy describes what data the game collects when you create an account and play, and how that data is used.

Data we collect

  • Email address and password — collected when you create an account, used only to sign you in and to let you recover your account. Passwords are stored hashed; we never see them in plain text.
  • Game progress — your save data (resources, crafted items, research progress, workers) is synced to our servers so you can continue playing across devices.
  • Tournament scores — if you participate in tournaments, your scores and submissions are stored to run leaderboards and detect cheating.
  • Purchase records — if you make an in-app purchase, we record which product you bought so we can grant and restore your entitlements. Payment itself is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive your card or billing details.

Data we do not collect

From Wood contains no advertising and no advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any device identifiers beyond what is technically required to run the game.

Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting (account and game data).
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase validation and entitlement management.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — payment processing for in-app purchases.

These providers process data only on our behalf to operate the game.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time — this permanently removes your email, credentials, game progress, and tournament history. See how to delete your account. Purchase transaction records are retained by Google Play or Apple according to their own policies.

Children

From Wood is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the account email required to play.
